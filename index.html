FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. The system works with the number you already have and advertise. Customers see texts from your business — nothing changes for them except that they hear back fast.

How much does it cost?

It's a simple monthly, from $1,000/mo, and one saved job usually more than covers it. New shops start with a free pilot — we build and run the whole system, the setup is waived, and the monthly doesn't start until it catches you a real missed-call job.

How long does setup take?

About a week. We handle the build; you just point us at your number and forwarding, and we test it together before it goes live.

What if it doesn't work for my shop?

You start with a free pilot, and everything is month-to-month — cancel anytime. If it's not catching jobs, you're not locked in and you don't keep paying.

Is it hard for my team to use?

No. It runs automatically in the background. The text-backs send themselves; your team just answers the customers who reply, the same way they always have.

Is the texting compliant?

Yes — the system is registered with the carriers (A2P 10DLC) and built to follow TCPA rules, with opt-out (reply STOP) handled automatically.
